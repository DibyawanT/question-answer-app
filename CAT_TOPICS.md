# CAT topic map (2023–2025)

Format since 2024: 3 sections × 40 min, 68 questions (VARC 24, DILR 22, QA 22). MCQ +3/−1, type-in-answer (TITA) +3/0.
Counts are my per-paper estimates from the pattern. Check them against the real papers: [Cracku](https://cracku.in/cat-previous-papers/) · [2IIM](https://online.2iim.com/CAT-question-paper/) · free official mock: [iimcat.ac.in](https://iimcat.ac.in).

## QA — 22 questions

| Topic | Qs per paper | Priority | Free resource |
|---|---|---|---|

## DILR — 22 questions (4–5 sets of 4–6)

| Set type | How often | Free resource |
|---|---|---|
| Arrangements & scheduling (floors, seats, days) | Most papers | [Puzzle Baron logic grids](https://logic.puzzlebaron.com/) |
| Tables/charts with missing values | Most papers | [Khan: arithmetic](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/arithmetic) (fast calculation) |
| Games & tournaments, scoring | Often | [Simon Tatham's puzzles](https://www.chiark.greenend.org.uk/~sgtatham/puzzles/) |
| Routes/networks, Venn/sets | Sometimes | same as above |

Pick sets, don't attempt them in order: read all of them in 4 minutes, then solve the 2–3 best.
